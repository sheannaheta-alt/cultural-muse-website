About the creator

Kia ora, I'm Sheanna

I'm Sheanna Heta, the creator behind Cultural Muse, based in Brisbane. This is my space for contemporary art, music and storytelling about connection, memory, mindfulness and belonging.

For the past three years this community has grown around cultural connection, nostalgia, uplifting messages and visual storytelling. The name is evolving, but the care and connection remain.

Every piece begins with a feeling, a memory or an observation. I choose the direction, make the creative and cultural decisions, and curate and approve everything that's shared — and everything you'll be able to take home.

Questions

Good to know

Is Cultural Muse an AI art page?

No. Cultural Muse is a human-led creative practice by Sheanna Heta. AI may assist in the background, but Sheanna directs, edits, curates and approves the final work.

Why did the name change?

The name is evolving as the work becomes more personal, culturally grounded and focused on art and products. It's still the same page and the same creator.

Does AI determine the cultural meaning?

No. Cultural meaning and cultural decisions are not delegated to a tool. Sheanna remains responsible for the creative and cultural judgement, and seeks trusted guidance where needed.

How do digital downloads work?

Digital products are delivered electronically after purchase. Full file sizes and formats will be listed on each product before release.

Do you ship to New Zealand and Australia?

That's the plan. Delivery locations, timing and costs will be confirmed and shared before physical products go on sale.
